Our Story

From Positano
to Vienna.

i Mulini was born from a place close to our hearts: Piazza dei Mulini in Positano.

For family and friends, the piazza has always been a meeting point — the place behind the simple phrase “Ci vediamo ai Mulini.”

That feeling of meeting, sharing and enjoying time together travelled with us from the Amalfi Coast to Vienna. Here in Josefstadt, i Mulini brings together Italian coffee, Mediterranean flavours, freshly prepared food and the ritual of aperitivo.

Giorgio brings the flavours and coffee culture of Naples, while Robert brings a life shaped between Austria and Positano and his passion for cocktails and aperitivo.
