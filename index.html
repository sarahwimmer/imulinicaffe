Our Story

From Italy
to Vienna.

i Mulini is a Mediterranean café in Vienna, bringing together Italian coffee, breakfast, food and aperitivo.

A place to meet over a cappuccino, share a focaccia, enjoy a glass of wine and stay a little longer.

Ci vediamo ai Mulini.
