The people behind i Mulini

Our Story.

Inspired by Piazza dei Mulini in Positano, a familiar meeting place for family and friends, i Mulini brings a little Mediterranean spirit to the heart of Vienna.

Founded by Giorgio, originally from Naples, and Robert, born in Vienna with a heart deeply connected to Positano, our café brings together their shared love for Italian hospitality.

Giorgio brings his passion for Italian coffee and cuisine, while Robert shares his love for aperitivo and cocktails.
